Put duaa recordings (MP3) in this folder. They appear in the app automatically:
select a duaa and they're listed in its Audio list.

Name rules: lowercase letters, numbers and _ only. No spaces, dashes or capitals.

Names that link the audio to a duaa:
  duaa_full.mp3      Travel duaa (full) - Sahih Muslim 1342
  duaa_riding.mp3    Duaa for getting in the car - Abu Dawud 2602
  duaa_short.mp3     Short version - Az-Zukhruf 43:13-14

More than one recording of the same duaa, e.g. different reciters:
  duaa_full_mishary.mp3   is listed as "Mishary" under Travel duaa (full)

Any other name, e.g. my_duaa.mp3, is listed under "My own recording" as "My duaa".

This note file is ignored by the app.
